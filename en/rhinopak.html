Rhinopak

At SOFT PACK, we understand that heavy-duty packaging requires more than just strength; it demands innovation and reliability. Rhinopak®, our new brand name for the enhanced corrugated board structure, embodies these principles, offering unparalleled durability and protection. Rhinopak® is designed to meet your toughest packaging challenges with ease and efficiency.
Let us introduce you to the robust and versatile Rhinopak®.

What is rhinopak?
Rhinopak® is an enhanced board corrugated structure designed for heavy-duty packaging applications. Engineered to provide superior strength and durability, Rhinopak® is ideal for industries that require the utmost in protection and performance.
Why Choose rhinopak?

Thickness up to 7.5mm

For enhanced protection from impact and puncture for even the heaviest items.

Various Profiles

Available in single-wall or double-wall.

Edge Crush Test (ECT) up to 21 KN/m

To safeguard your products during transit and extended storage.

Bursting Strength up to 2,430 kPa

To provide side pressure bearing capacity and minimise damage risks.

Grammage (GSM) up to 1,000

Enhancing the rigidity and strength of the packaging.

Very Flat Sheets

Expertly designed and constructed guaranteeing flat boards.

High Grammage Kraft Liner

Recyclable virgin fibres further boost the overall strength and integrity of the packaging.

Easy to Set Up

Allowing for efficient assembly and use.

Multiple Fluting Combinations

Such as CA, BC, EB, A, C, and more.

Lighter Than Other Materials

Like wood, steel, or plastic.

Printable

Up to 8 colours using various printing methods, be it flexographic Preprint, Flexographic Direct Print, Or Digital.
