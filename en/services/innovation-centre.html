Innovation Centre

Our belief in the future of paper-based packaging, and its contributions in facing today's environmental challenges, inspired us to create our Innovation Centre in order to enhance your packaging by thinking outside the box.

What Is The Innovation Centre?
“The Innovation Centre” is a team of Packaging and Logistic experts in a dedicated infrastructure equipped with design and simulation software, prototyping and testing tools, equipment, and easy access to a fully-fledged manufacturing operation for advanced trials and pilot projects. It creates value by finding unconventional solutions to problems and aligning synergies in order to explore technological availabilities, structural design enhancement, manufacturing, and logistics optimization opportunities.

It is a cluster of creative consultants, from structural designers and 3D animators to graphic designers and market researchers. They work together, harmoniously, to develop and create the ideal packaging solutions that will stand out from the competitors and add real value to your brand.
Collaborating with our innovation centre guarantees packaging solutions that will turn your products from inconspicuous to outstanding on the shelves.
